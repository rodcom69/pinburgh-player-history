Method: pulled from the same dataset as the Pinburgh Results Lookup tool — every player-year scraped directly from pinburgh.com's live results pages, 2014–2026. "Day 1 · whole field" is each player's standing across the entire field after Session 5 (the point pinburgh.com locks in and publishes on the transition page, used to assign Day 2 divisions). "Day 2 · whole field" is their final standing across the entire field after Session 10. Both are whole-field, so they're on the same scale; "Places gained" is the difference. Limited to players whose win + loss total equals exactly 120 (the full 10-session event) with no absent session, so no one who withdrew appears here. Players tied on places gained share the same rank. Because a big climb needs room below you to start, this board naturally favours players seeded into the lower Day 2 divisions after a middling Day 1.
